Credits

This site is hand-coded HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no framework, no tracking. The drawings, icons, paper texture and typefaces come from these open projects.

  • Open PeepsCC0

    The hand-drawn busts of the older learners, their teacher and the banjo-playing grandpa are Open Peeps by Pablo Stanley, released under CC0 and assembled through the DiceBear API.

  • OpenMojiCC BY-SA 4.0

    The small line icons — keyboard, guitar, banjo, headphones and score — are from OpenMoji. “All emojis designed by OpenMoji – the open-source emoji and icon project.” Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0. The icons were recoloured to the page ink; those adaptations are shared under the same licence.

  • Open DoodlesCC0

    The dancing figure with headphones on the “page not found” page is Open Doodles by Pablo Stanley (CC0), recoloured to match the palette.

  • Subtle Patterns — “Paper Fibers”CC BY-SA 3.0

    The faint paper grain behind these pages is the Paper Fibers texture by Heliodor Jalba, from Subtle Patterns (Atle Mo), CC BY-SA 3.0, converted to a low-alpha overlay.

  • LucideISC

    Interface icons (mail, arrows, document, download) are from Lucide, ISC licence. © Lucide Contributors; portions © 2013–2022 Cole Bemis (Feather, MIT). Permission to use, copy, modify and distribute is granted provided this notice appears.

  • rough.jsMIT

    The sketchy frames around the photo, cards and buttons were drawn once at build time with rough.js (MIT) and inlined as static SVG — nothing runs in the browser.

  • TypefacesSIL OFL 1.1

    Fraunces by Undercase Type, Kalam by the Indian Type Foundry and Caveat by Pablo Impallari, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and self-hosted here. Licence texts: OFL-fraunces.txt · OFL-kalam.txt · OFL-caveat.txt
